# Smart Parking Platform - Risk Management

## Risk Identification
Risks were identified in the Smart Parking Platform and are organized into categories from technical, schedule, financial, and people-related risks.

### Technical Risks
1. Third-party payment services failure.
2. Navigational API failure.
3. Incorrect real-time parking availability.
4. Security vulnerabilities that involve a user or user payment information.
### Schedule Risks
1. Tasks for the development could take longer than the estimated time.
2. Delays may occur due to integration of third-party services.
3. Development teams becoming dependent on one another may cause delays.
4. Testing may take longer than originally planned.
### Financial Risks
1. Third-party API costs could become costly during development.
2. Cloud hosting costs could become costly during development.
3. Development teams may need different software or services leading to unexpected expenses.
4. Additional development costs may arise due to project delays.
### People-Related Risks
1. Development team availability may cause delays.
2. Communication issues may arise with development teams.
3. Lack of experience with required technologies may cause issues.
4. Poor distribution between developmental work may cause project delays.

## Risk Register

### R-01: Third-Party Payment Services Failure
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** High
- **Owner:** Payment Integration Team
- **Response Strategy:** Monitor all payment services and have an alternative payment option available for users if needed. 
- **Status / Notes:** Open- Monitoring the payment services' availability.

### R-02: Navigational API Failure
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Owner:** Navigation Services Team
- **Response Strategy:** Monitor the navigation API and have an alternative navigation service available for users to use if needed.
- **Status / Notes:** Open - Monitor navigation API availability.

### R-03: Incorrect Real-Time Parking Availability
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** High
- **Owner:** Backend/API Team
- **Response Strategy:** Monitor the real-time parking availability by regularly testing and validating the availbility data to ensure accurate information is being displayed to users.
- **Status / Notes:** Open - Monitor parking availability accuracy and failures.

### R-04: Security Vulnerabilities Involving User or User's Payment Information
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** High
- **Owner:** Payment Integration Team
- **Response Strategy:** Utilize secure authentication, encryption, and security testing to ensure the user and the users payment information is protected.
- **Status / Notes:** Open - Monitor and test for vulnerabilities.

### R-05: Development Tasks May Take Longer Than Originally Estimated
- **Probability:** High
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Owner:** Project Manager
- **Response Strategy:** Monitor the task progress and adjust the project schedule when tasks are taking longer than estimated due to project delays.
- **Status / Notes:** Open - Review the development progress weekly to ensure project completion.

### R-06: Delays Due to Integration of Third-Party Services
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Owner:** Backend/API Team
- **Response Strategy:** Begin integrating third-party services early and test integrations throughout the developmental process.
- **Status / Notes:** Open - Monitor the progress of the third-party integration.

### R-07: Dependencies Between Development Teams May Cause Delays
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Owner:** Project Manager
- **Response Strategy:** Coordinate tasks between development teams and identify any conflicts or dependencies before the development begins.
- **Status / Notes:** Open - Review the dependencies between the teams regularly and adjust to minimize project delays.

### R-08: Testing May Take Longer Than Originally Planned
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Owner:** QA & Testing Team
- **Response Strategy:** Begin testing development early to allow additional time for testing when neccessary. 
- **Status / Notes:** Open - Monitor the testing progress.

### R-09: Third-Party API Costs Could Increase During Development
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Owner:** Backend/API Team

- **Impact:** High
