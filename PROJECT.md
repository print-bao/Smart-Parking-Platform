## Project Overview
$oftware ¢orp. is a fictional software development company working on the Smart Parking Platform. The purpose of this project is to plan and manage the development of a system that will help drivers locate, reserve, and pay for available parking spaces in real time.

The Smart Parking Platform will be available through both web and mobile applications. It will provide drivers with parking availability and navigation assistance while also giving parking operators tools to monitor and manage their parking facilities.

The overall goal of this project is to create a project management plan that covers requirements, stakeholders, scheduling, budgeting, risk management, and coordination between the different development teams.

## Business Need

Drivers can spend a lot of time searching for available parking, especially in busy areas. This can cause more traffic congestion, frustration, and wasted time for drivers. At the same time, parking operators need better ways to monitor parking occupancy, manage pricing, and make better use of their available parking spaces.

The Smart Parking Platform is intended to address these problems by making it easier for drivers to find and reserve available parking while also providing parking operators with tools to better manage their parking facilities.

## High-Level Requirements

The Smart Parking Platform should provide the following:

- User registration and authentication
- Real-time parking space availability
- Interactive map showing available parking locations
- Ability for users to reserve parking spaces
- Digital payment processing
- Reservation history and receipts
- Administrative dashboard for parking operators
- Occupancy reporting and analytics
- Notifications and alerts for users
- Integration with external mapping and navigation services

These are the initial high-level requirements for the platform. Additional requirements will be identified later through stakeholder analysis and requirements elicitation.

## Stakeholders

The main stakeholders involved with the Smart Parking Platform include:

- Drivers and vehicle owners
- Parking facility operators
- City transportation departments
- System administrators
- Finance and billing personnel
- Mobile application users
- Third-party payment providers

Each stakeholder group has a different role or interest in the project. Drivers and mobile users will mainly interact with the platform to find and reserve parking, while parking operators and administrators will use the system to manage parking facilities and monitor operations. Finance personnel and third-party payment providers will be involved with payment-related functions, while city transportation departments may have an interest in parking operations and transportation management.

## Development Teams

The Smart Parking Platform will be divided among multiple development teams, with each team responsible for a different part of the system:

- Mobile Application Team
- Web Application Team
- Backend/API Team
- Mapping & Location Services Team
- Payment Integration Team
- Quality Assurance & Testing Team

These teams will work together throughout the project to make sure the different parts of the Smart Parking Platform are properly developed, integrated, and tested.

## Project Constraints

The Smart Parking Platform has the following project constraints:

- Unlimited theoretical development budget
- Fixed semester timeline
- Dependence on third-party services and APIs
- Compliance with security and privacy requirements, city ordinances, and laws

Although the project has an unlimited theoretical budget, it still needs to be planned and completed within the semester timeline. The platform will also depend on external services, such as mapping and payment services, which will need to be considered during the planning and development of the project.

## Research Component

As part of the Smart Parking Platform project, research will be conducted on existing parking applications and services. The purpose of this research is to understand what current parking solutions already provide and identify ways that the Smart Parking Platform could be different.

The competitive analysis will focus on:

- Features offered by existing parking platforms
- Strengths of each existing solution
- Weaknesses or limitations of each solution
- Opportunities for the Smart Parking Platform to improve on existing solutions
- Features that could help differentiate the Smart Parking Platform

The results of the competitive analysis will be used to help refine the project's requirements and overall scope.
